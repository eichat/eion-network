The plan

The wallet and the exchange of coins for the EION token are already built into the app — and they work. For now on a Solana test network: there the token has no value and can be neither bought nor sold. Whether a real launch happens depends on resources. There are no dates.

Part of every payment to the platform is burned for good — 30% on calls and uploads, 40% on the AI assistant and sticker packs, 50% on premium. Burned coins leave circulation and land in no account.

Today coins are an internal accounting unit of the service. They are not money, securities or cryptocurrency, cannot be exchanged for money, and this page is not an offer to invest.
