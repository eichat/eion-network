The plan

A long-term goal is to take EION coins beyond the app — as a token on Solana. That work has not started, and no dates are promised.

Part of every payment to the platform is burned for good — 30% on calls and uploads, 40% on the AI assistant and sticker packs, 50% on premium. Burned coins leave circulation and land in no account.

Today EION coins are an internal accounting unit of the service. They are not money, securities or cryptocurrency, cannot be exchanged for money, and this page is not an offer to invest.
